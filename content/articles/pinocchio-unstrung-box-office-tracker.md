A Nightmarish Fairy Tale

Jude-Evan Lloyd's Pinocchio: Unstrung presents a grim and gory reimagining of the classic children's story. Directed by Rhys Frake-Waterfield, this 82-minute horror-fantasy follows the puppet Pinocchio, who, influenced by a sinister Cricket and created by Geppetto, launches a violent crusade. His goal is to carve himself into a real boy like his brother James, one piece at a time, weaving a dark mystery into the familiar narrative.

## A Cast of Horror Icons and New Faces

The film's ensemble is led by Jude Evan Lloyd in the titular role of Pinocchio. He is joined by horror legend Robert Englund, who voices the malevolent Cricket. Richard Brake plays the creator Geppetto, while Cameron Bell portrays Pinocchio's brother, James. The supporting cast includes Jessica Balmer as Mia, Jack Art Gray as Dudley, Scott Chambers as Christopher Robin, and Kelly Rian Sanson as Miss Daniels, creating a world where childhood innocence is brutally subverted.
