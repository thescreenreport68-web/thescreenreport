[Guillermo del Toro](/movies/guillermo-del-toro-bans-ai-for-pan-s-labyrinth-3d-re-release-citing-a/) announced at San Diego Comic-Con on July 24 that the upcoming 3D re-release of his classic film *Pan's Labyrinth* was created with one non-negotiable rule: no artificial intelligence. The director revealed he personally oversaw the painstaking, frame-by-frame conversion by hand for the film's 20th anniversary return to theaters this October. His declaration, met with applause from the Hall H audience, was a pointed statement on artistic integrity in an industry increasingly flirting with automation.

Del Toro's stance is a continuation of his long-held, vocal opposition to generative AI in filmmaking. According to reports from Variety and NME, he framed the decision as a defense of the artistic process itself. "What we're protecting is the beauty and the redeeming power of art," del Toro said. "It's not about who gets the job. We are protecting a lineage of art. If we cut a generation of people from learning their craft, you're cutting the rest of the history of that medium away from them for what?"

## What Are the Details of the Re-Release?

The 20th anniversary event brings the dark fantasy back to the big screen with several firsts and exclusive offerings. Here are the key details confirmed for the re-release:

- **Release Date:** The film returns to theaters on **October 9, 2026**
- **Formats:** It will be presented in both **3D and 2D High Dynamic Range (HDR)** for the first time, in a 4K restoration overseen by del Toro
- **Distributors:** The theatrical presentations are being handled by **Cineverse and Fathom**
- **Related Project:** Del Toro also teased a new documentary about the making of the original film, featuring commentary from **Jacob Elordi, Cate Blanchett, and Charlie Day**

Del Toro emphasized that the hand-crafted approach was a deliberate choice of quality over speed. "I chose good," he stated. "You have to put in time. You have to do every element by hand. A human made a decision of depth."

## Why Is 'Pan's Labyrinth' Such a Landmark Film?

First released in 2006, *Pan's Labyrinth* is widely regarded as a masterpiece of dark fantasy and one of del Toro's defining works. The film, set in post-Civil War Spain in 1944, follows young Ofelia (Ivana Baquero) as she escapes her cruel stepfather's world into a mysterious labyrinth overseen by a faun. It seamlessly blends brutal historical reality with terrifying fairy-tale mythology.

The film was a critical and awards juggernaut. It boasts a 95% critics score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 98/100 on Metacritic. At the Academy Awards, it won three Oscars for Best Cinematography, Best Art Direction, and Best Makeup, out of six total nominations. Commercially, it was also a success, grossing $83 million worldwide against a production budget of $19 million. Its legacy includes a legendary 22-minute standing ovation at its Cannes Film Festival premiere, a record at the time.

## How Does This Fit Into Del Toro's Broader AI Stance?

This is not the first time del Toro has drawn a hard line against AI.

His [Comic-Con](/movies/san-diego-comic-con-2026-a-celebration-of-comics-or-a-digital-pop-culture/) comments, therefore, put principle into practice. By investing the time and expense into a manual 3D conversion for a beloved classic, he is making a tangible argument for the value of human artisanship. It positions the re-release not just as a nostalgia trip, but as a statement piece in the ongoing debate about technology's role in art preservation and creation.

## What's Next for the 'Pan's Labyrinth' Anniversary?

The documentary featuring Elordi, Blanchett, and Day remains teased without a specific release date or platform announced. The 20th anniversary celebration also included a fan-focused "Fetch Quest" scavenger hunt at Comic-Con, offering limited-edition posters and a private signing with del Toro.

With the re-release, a new generation will experience the film's haunting beauty as del Toro intended—crafted entirely by human hands, a testament to the artistic lineage he is determined to protect.
