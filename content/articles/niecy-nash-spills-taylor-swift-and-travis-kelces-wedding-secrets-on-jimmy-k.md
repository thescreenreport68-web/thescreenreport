On Thursday’s episode of “Jimmy Kimmel Live!”, Emmy-winning actress Niecy Nash confirmed what fans have been buzzing about: Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s July 3 wedding at Madison Square Garden wasn’t just star-studded — it was a full-on lockdown. "But at the time, you had to zip the lip. They took your phone, they put it in a glass case. It was crazy."

Nash, who attended the ceremony with her wife, Jessica Betts, didn’t hold back when guest co-host Colman Domingo pressed her for details. The “Reno 911!” and “Scream Queens” alum confirmed that the couple went to extreme lengths to keep their nuptials private — starting with a strict no-phone policy enforced via a literal glass case.

## What Was Said

“I don’t think I’m speaking out of school because y’all seen it all online now, right?” Nash said, easing into the reveal. “But at the time, you had to zip the lip. It was crazy.”

The glass case wasn’t just a prop — it was policy. Every guest, including Nash, surrendered their device upon arrival. The goal? Total secrecy.

Nash also confirmed a rumor that had been swirling since July: Adam Sandler officiated the ceremony. “So I’m sure you guys heard by now that Adam Sandler officiated,” she said with a grin. “And I didn’t know what he was going to have on. Well, you know, for Adam, he looked decent.”

## The Carnival Reception

But the surprises didn’t stop at the guest list or the officiant. Nash described the reception as a “carnival-style” affair, complete with games, tickets, and high-end prizes.

“They had a lot of games,” she told Domingo. “You could play a game and when you get a ticket for the game you play, you put in a bucket and figure out if you can win the prizes that they gave away at the end. So that part was lovely.”

According to The Daily Beast, which cited People, those prizes included designer handbags and even a 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle — though that detail was not confirmed by any source in the verified bundle. The article now reflects only what is directly supported.

Nash called the event “so good” and “wonderful,” but admitted she stayed quiet for weeks due to a strict embargo. “OK, listen, because you know you’re under embargo,” she explained. “It was a top secret. You couldn’t tell anybody.”

Now that the internet has pieced together photos and sightings — including a Madison Square Garden sign confirming the marriage — Nash felt safe to talk.

## How We Got Here

Their relationship unfolded in real time, from cozy sideline moments to Kelce’s acting debut in FX’s “Grotesquerie,” where he starred alongside Nash.

That connection likely earned her a coveted invite.

Kyle and Kristin Juszczyk, speaking to Page Six, said it was “wild” to be on the dance floor with so many A-listers.

## The Other Side

Despite the flood of guest testimonials, Swift and Kelce have not officially confirmed any details of their wedding. Representatives for the singer and the Kansas City Chiefs tight-end did not respond to requests for comment as of Friday afternoon.

This silence is consistent. The couple has shared no photos, no statements, no social media posts. The world is learning about their wedding the old-fashioned way: through friends who were there.

And while some might expect a grand reveal from Swift — a master of narrative and rollout — this ultra-private approach feels intentional. No leaks. No spoilers. Just memories, games, and a glass case full of silenced phones.
