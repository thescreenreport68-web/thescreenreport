The Rookie creator Alexi Hawley announced at San Diego Comic-Con that the show's ninth season will debut with a two-part premiere in January 2027. The news, reported by Deadline, addresses the shocking Season 8 finale cliffhanger that left fans reeling. Hawley and the main cast appeared at the panel to tease the upcoming season and apologize for the dramatic twist.

Production on the new season began the week of the convention, with star Nathan Fillion set to join his colleagues on set the following Monday. The series, which first aired in 2018, follows John Nolan, a middle-aged man who becomes the LAPD's oldest rookie. It has been nominated for five Primetime Emmys, according to IMDb, and currently streams on Hulu, fuboTV, Philo, and YouTube TV.

## What Will the Season 9 Premiere Be Like?

Hawley provided specifics about the supersized premiere during the panel. He described it as a major event that will not resolve everything in a single hour.

"It's a two-parter, so it won't all be resolved in one episode — there's a lot going on," Hawley said, according to Deadline. "I think it's the best of what we do, it's incredibly high stakes, dramatic. It's a love story, it's funny and unexpected."

The key details for Season 9 include:

- **Premiere Format:** A two-part opening episode
- **Release Window:** January 2027 on ABC
- **Story Focus:** Picking up from the Season 8 cliffhanger
- **Production Start:** Began in July 2026
- **Spinoff Launch:** *The Rookie: North* will also premiere in January 2027

## The Cast Apologizes for the Season 8 Cliffhanger

The panel heavily addressed the Season 8 finale, which aired on May 4. In the final moments, Tim proposed to Lucy on a beach at sunset, only for both characters to be immediately kidnapped. Nathan Fillion asked the Comic-Con audience how many "had a conniption in their living room" watching the scene, prompting a sea of raised hands.

Melissa O'Neil, who plays Lucy, expressed sympathy for the fans. "Oh no! We will send you some magnesium to calm you down, have a nice little bit of tea," she joked. "We're so sorry for that, but also thank you for going along for the ride."

O'Neil also shared her reaction to reading the script. "Honestly, when I was reading it the first time it was like, 'I can't believe he's finally giving it to them, I'm in shock,'" she told the crowd. "And then the next moment I was like, 'See? I knew he wasn't going to give it to them.'"

## How the Finale Scene Was Filmed

O'Neil revealed that filming the pivotal beach scene was a rushed affair. "It was very rushed to shoot that day, though, it was very unceremonious. We were chasing the light," she said.

Hawley had previously hinted to Deadline in May about how Season 9 would pick up. "We're definitely gonna start that way, figure out where they are, and figure out how to get them back, and whatever obstacles we can put in their way on that," he said. "And then seeing Tim and Lucy wherever they are, I don't think they're going to sit idly by and wait to be rescued."

## What's Next for The Rookie Franchise?

With production now underway, the next confirmed milestone is the January 2027 premiere on ABC. The season will launch alongside the new spinoff, *The Rookie: North*, starring Jay Ellis, which was also promoted at Comic-Con. Ellis crashed the main show's panel to ask the veteran cast for advice.

Fillion offered a practical tip: "Learn everybody's name. It make is much easier to apologize if ever you are late." Other cast members suggested stretching before stunts and using knee pads. The interconnected premieres set up a major January for ABC, with the mothership series tackling its most intense cliffhanger yet.
