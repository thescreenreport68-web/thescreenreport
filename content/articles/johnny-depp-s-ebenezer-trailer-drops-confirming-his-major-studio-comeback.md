Johnny Depp made a surprise appearance at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2026, fully in character as Ebenezer Scrooge, to personally unveil the first official trailer for his upcoming film. The theatrical stunt and trailer release mark a definitive push to re-establish Depp as a leading man in Hollywood after several quiet years.

The appearance was a full-scale marketing event. Deadline reported that Paramount erected a faux 1880s British Christmas village in San Diego’s Gaslamp district, complete with Victorian carolers. Depp emerged from the installation, staying in character—complete with a nose prosthetic—to address the crowd. He responded to shouts of “We love you” with grumbles and a final, quiet “Humbug” before departing. The moment was a calculated zag in Comic-Con marketing, proving a major splash could be made outside the traditional Hall H panel.

## What Is 'Ebenezer' About?

‘Ebenezer’ is a new adaptation of Charles Dickens’ ‘A Christmas Carol.’ The official synopsis describes the tale of an elderly miser, Ebenezer Scrooge, who embarks on a supernatural journey after being visited by the ghost of his former business partner and the Spirits of Christmas Past, Present, and Yet to Come. The journey forces him to confront his past and fight for a second chance at happiness. The film is categorized as a Drama, Fantasy, and Comedy, with thematic keywords including redemption, gothic fantasy, ghost, and Victorian era.

**The confirmed cast and their roles are:**
* Johnny Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge
* Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit
* Andrea Riseborough as the Ghost of Christmas Past
* Tramell Tillman as the Ghost of Christmas Present
* Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley
* Daisy Ridley as Emily Cratchit
* Sam Claflin as Fred
* Arthur Conti as Young Ebenezer
* Ellie Bamber as Isabel Fezziwig

The film is scheduled for a US theatrical release on **November 13, 2026**.

## The Director and the Cast

Director Ti West, best known for his acclaimed horror trilogy ‘X,’ ‘Pearl,’ and ‘MaXXXine,’ brings a genre-savvy eye to the classic story. His involvement suggests the potential for a darker, more stylized take on the Dickensian tale, a notion supported by the film’s ‘gothic fantasy’ and ‘dark fantasy’ tags. The cast blends franchise veterans with character actors. Alongside Depp, the ensemble features ‘Harry Potter’ alum Rupert Grint, legendary stage and screen actor Ian McKellen, and Daisy Ridley.

The A.V. Club noted the thematic savvy of casting Depp, who maintains his innocence regarding past abuse allegations, in the archetypal story of a bad man learning to be better. The trailer itself leans into Depp's legacy, explicitly naming his iconic roles—Jack Sparrow, Edward Scissorhands, the Mad Hatter—before declaring “the master of misfits returns.” The final shot reportedly features Depp whispering, “It’s good to be back,” directly to the camera.

## Why This Signals a Comeback

For Depp, this project represents a clear re-entry into the mainstream studio system. Following his 2022 defamation trial with ex-wife Amber Heard, his film output consisted of smaller projects like ‘Minamata’ and the French period piece ‘Jeanne du Barry.’ ‘Ebenezer’ is a tentpole holiday release from a major studio, backed by a significant marketing budget evident in the elaborate Comic-Con activation. The Guardian observed that the actor has “struggled to reclaim his position in Hollywood” in recent years, making this high-profile campaign a bold statement of return.

The public reaction to the trailer, as reported, has been largely positive in online spaces, with many commenters expressing excitement to see Depp back on screen. However, the promotional framing has also drawn scrutiny for its attempt to rehabilitate his public image through the lens of a redemption narrative. The success of this comeback bid will ultimately be measured at the box office when the film opens in November 2026.

## What Happens Next?

With the trailer now public and the release date set, the next confirmed milestone is the film’s theatrical debut on November 13, 2026. Paramount will likely continue its marketing campaign through the fall, potentially with additional footage or promotional events. The true test of Depp’s Hollywood comeback will be whether audiences flock to the theaters for his return as the quintessential Christmas curmudgeon seeking a second chance.
