Prime Video has dropped the first official trailer for *[Reacher](/tv/prime-video-drops-the-official-reacher-season-4-trailer-and-a-neagley/)* Season 4, confirming the show’s return for a bloody new adventure this August. The footage debuted during the show’s panel at San Diego Comic-Con, according to reports from ComingSoon and /Film. The new season will adapt one of Lee Child’s wildest novels, *Gone Tomorrow*, and will be followed immediately by the launch of a new spin-off series.

Alan Ritchson is back as the formidable drifter, Jack Reacher, a veteran military police investigator now living off the grid. The series, created by Nick Santora, has been a cornerstone of Amazon’s action lineup since its 2022 debut.

## What Are the Key Details for Season 4?

The trailer confirms the major plot points, premiere strategy, and new faces joining the fray. Here are the concrete facts from the official announcements.

* **Premiere Date:** Wednesday, August 12, 2026, on Prime Video.
* **Release Schedule:** The first three episodes drop simultaneously, with the remaining five releasing weekly.
* **Source Material:** The season adapts *Gone Tomorrow*, the 13th book in Lee Child’s bestselling series.
* **Official Synopsis:** "When a chance encounter with a distraught stranger on a subway goes horribly wrong, Jack Reacher (Alan Ritchson) is drawn into a complex and deadly game that pits him against ruthless foes from the highest echelons of power."
* **Key New Cast:** Sydelle Noel, Christopher Rodriguez Marquette, Kevin Weisman, Marc Blucas, Kevin Corrigan, and Kathleen Robertson.
* **Season Finale:** September 16, 2026.

## What Is the Story of 'Gone Tomorrow'?

Season 4 pivots from the stately home siege of Season 3 to the gritty public transit of New York City. The plot kicks off with a seemingly random tragedy. Reacher witnesses a woman commit suicide on a subway train, an event the NYPD is eager to write off. His instincts tell him otherwise. His investigation reveals the woman was a civilian Pentagon clerk entangled in a massive, multi-layered cover-up. This pulls Reacher into a dangerous world involving federal agents and Russian operatives, with roots stretching back to the Cold War and the Soviet-Afghan conflict. As Ritchson previously promised, this season is positioned to be the bloodiest yet, trading a single physical titan like Season 3's Paulie for a more insidious, powerful network of enemies.

## Who Is Joining the Cast and Crew?

The new season introduces a fresh roster of characters caught in the conspiracy. Christopher Rodriguez Marquette plays Jacob Merrick, while Sydelle Noel takes on the role of Tamara Green. Agnez Mo appears as Lila Hoth, and Kevin Corrigan joins as Detective Dochert. They round out a cast led by Ritchson’s physically imposing yet cerebral Jack Reacher. The series continues under the guidance of showrunner Nick Santora, who has shepherded the show from its first season. Filming for Season 4 wrapped in March 2026, as reported by MovieWeb, setting the stage for this summer’s release.

## How Does This Expand the 'Reacher' Universe?

The launch of Season 4 is just one part of a larger strategic push by Prime Video. The season finale on September 16, 2026, will be immediately followed by the debut of *Neagley*, a spin-off series. All eight episodes of that show will drop at once for a binge release. Starring Maria Sten as Frances Neagley, a private investigator and former military protégé of Reacher, the series will see her hunting for justice after a friend’s suspicious death. Alan Ritchson is set to make a guest appearance. This move mirrors Amazon’s successful expansion of franchises like *The Boys*, building a connected universe of action shows. Furthermore, the main *Reacher* series is not slowing down. Prime Video has already renewed it for a fifth season, with work reportedly underway, ensuring Ritchson’s hero will continue his wanderings for the foreseeable future.

## What Happens Next?

With the trailer now public, the countdown to August 12 is officially on. Fans can expect a weekly rollout of episodes through mid-September. The immediate follow-up is the September 16 launch of *Neagley*, which will test the waters for a broader franchise. The confirmed renewal for Season 5 means this is far from the last we’ll see of Jack Reacher. For now, the stage is set for his most complex and conspiracy-driven case yet.
