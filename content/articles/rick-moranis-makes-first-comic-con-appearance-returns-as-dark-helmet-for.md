[Rick Moranis](/movies/rick-moranis-returns-for-comic-con-debut-joins-spaceballs-sequel-cast/) returned to the spotlight on July 24, 2026, making his first-ever appearance at San Diego Comic-Con for the upcoming sequel "Spaceballs: The New One." The 73-year-old actor, who has been largely absent from Hollywood for decades, received a thunderous standing ovation and chants of his name from the Hall H crowd.

He confirmed his return to the role of Dark Helmet in the film, which marks his first live-action movie role in nearly 30 years. "This came along right at the right time," Moranis said during the panel. "It just felt like, this is it. It's time, and yes. I said yes, and it is the best decision I've ever made."

## What Happened at the Comic-Con Panel?

The panel was a meta send-up of the event itself, complete with a parody of Marvel Studios' multi-phase cinematic universe timeline. According to Deadline, the presentation opened with a humorous timeline displayed on Hall H's wrap-around screens, outlining more than 11 imagined phases of "The Almighty Schwartz Saga."

Moranis made his entrance by hiding in the audience wearing his character's iconic oversized helmet, before being discovered by costumed soldiers using an equally oversized comb. His appearance triggered an extended standing ovation. The event also featured:

- A sneak peek at a teaser trailer that spoofs "Avatar," "[Star Wars](/movies/a-star-wars-powerwash-pack-has-fans-in-a-chokehold-but-not-for-the-reason-you/): The Rise of Skywalker," and franchise filmmaking
- Prerecorded video messages from original stars Mel Brooks, 100, and Bill Pullman
- Confirmation from [Josh Gad](/movies/josh-gad-to-join-margot-robbie-and-bradley-cooper-s-ocean-s-prequel-ex/) that he is playing a new "mawg" character, not replacing the late John Candy's Barf

## Who Is in the 'Spaceballs' Sequel Cast?

The sequel is a blend of legacy stars and new faces. Director Josh Greenbaum is helming the project from a screenplay by Josh Gad, Dan Hernandez, and Benji Samit.

Original cast members reprising their roles include Rick Moranis as Dark Helmet, Bill Pullman as Lone Starr, Daphne Zuniga as Queen Vespa, Mel Brooks as Yogurt, and George Wyner as Colonel Sandurz.

They are joined by franchise newcomers, including Lewis Pullman (playing Lone Starr's son, Starburst), Keke Palmer, Josh Gad, Anthony Carrigan, and Michael Winslow.

## Why Did Rick Moranis Take a Break From Hollywood?

Moranis's lengthy hiatus from major film roles was a deliberate choice rooted in family. After the death of his wife, costume designer Ann Belsky, from breast cancer in 1991, he prioritized raising their two children as a single father. He told The Hollywood Reporter in 2015, "I have absolutely no regrets whatsoever. My life is wonderful."

He never considered himself formally retired, continuing with voice work and comedy albums, but found the travel and schedule of live-action filmmaking incompatible with his parenting responsibilities. His last live-action film was the 1997 direct-to-video title "Honey, We Shrunk Ourselves!"

## What Is 'Spaceballs: The New One' About?

The film is a direct sequel to the 1987 cult classic spoof, set forty years later.

Josh Gad explained the film's thematic core, stating the team talked about "this word nostalgia" and how "'Spaceballs' is the perfect vehicle to catch us up on everything that's happened in filmdom, and in fan filmdom and pop culture, over the past 40 years."

## When Does the Film Come Out?

"Spaceballs: The New One" is scheduled for a wide theatrical release on April 23, 2027. The panel concluded with a sizzle reel that, while not released publicly, showcased the film's parody of modern blockbuster trends, from unfinished visual effects to the business of studio mergers.

## Josh Gad's Tribute to John Candy

Josh Gad clarified his role in the sequel, directly addressing fan speculation. He confirmed he is not replacing the late John Candy as Barf, a role he called singular. Gad stated the film is a 'love letter' to Candy, whom he described as one of his biggest idols.

He explained that John Candy is 'at the beating heart of this film,' contributing to its emotional core. Gad will instead play a new character from the same 'mawg' species as Barf, honoring the original while carving a new path.
