Tom Morris Jr. will return to the television series *On Patrol: Live* as a co-host and analyst on July 31, according to The Hollywood Reporter. He reunites with host Dan Abrams and retired Tulsa Police Department Sgt. Sean “Sticks” Larkin, with whom he previously worked on A&E’s *Live PD*.

Morris Jr. expressed his excitement in a statement. **“Reuniting with Dan and Sticks is so special for me! We have always been more to each other than just professional colleagues,”** he said. “We are truly friends for life and have remained in close contact over the years. I’m looking forward to kicking it on set with Curtis and Rizzo too. Onward and upward, OP Nation!”

## Who else is on the show?

The on-air team also includes analysts Captain Tom Rizzo (ret.) of the Howell Township Police Department and Deputy Sheriff Curtis Wilson of the Richland County Sheriff’s Department. Abrams welcomed Morris Jr.'s return, highlighting his unique contributions. “I am thrilled to be working with Tom again, not just for his law enforcement insight but because he brings humor and humanity to his analysis,” Abrams said. “It’s great to have the team back together.”

## When does the show air?

*On Patrol: Live* follows live news-gathering protocols with more than 50 cameras in communities across the country, documenting the nightly work of law enforcement officers in real time. The show airs every Friday and Saturday night from 9 p.m. to 12 a.m. ET on Reelz. *On Patrol: First Shift* provides recaps and updates before each live episode at 8 p.m. ET.

Morris Jr. is a former audio technician and field producer who became an antiterrorism contractor for the U.S. State Department. His television career includes work on *America’s Most Wanted*, A&E’s *Wanted* and *Fugitives Caught on Tape*, and appearances on several major news networks. He is also a Washington, D.C., Special Police Officer.
