Jessica Biel said she wishes she could shrink her sons and keep them in her pocket all day, a sentiment she shared during an exclusive interview with E! The 44-year-old actress, known for her role in *Bleeding Heart*, made the heartfelt remark while promoting her upcoming film *Matchbox*, set to hit theaters October 9.

She didn’t mince words when asked what—or who—she’d most like to carry with her at all times. "My kids!" she told E! News. "That would be great if they could just hang out right in a pocket, hanging with me all day long."

It was a moment of playful honesty from a mom who rarely speaks publicly about her family life. But when she does, it’s with warmth and intention. Her sons, Silas, 11, and Phineas, 5, are clearly at the center of her world—and not just because of her pocket fantasy.

## What Was Said

Biel’s comments at Comic-Con 2026 weren’t just about missing her kids. They painted a picture of a mother deeply in tune with the fleeting nature of childhood. While she adores her *Matchbox* costars—John Cena, Sam Richardson, and Arturo Castro—she made it clear that no amount of on-set camaraderie compares to time with her boys.

“We just had a good time and they’re so lovely and such team players,” she said of her castmates, calling the experience “a joy” and adding, “It was like not a bad egg in the bunch.”

Her affection for her husband, Justin Timberlake, also took center stage—though not at the event itself, but in a recent social media post. On June 21, Biel shared a Father’s Day tribute to the 45-year-old singer, captioning a photo: "Happy Father’s Day to the dad who is always ALL in,"

She went on to praise Timberlake’s hands-on parenting, writing that he turns “ordinary moments into adventures and bedtime into comedy hour,” and that he’s “not afraid of the tears, the tender, quiet times, or the hard conversations.”

“Watching you be a father is one of my favorite things,” she added. “Our boys are so lucky to call you Dad.”

Timberlake responded in kind, posting his own tribute the same day. “Every year this day rolls around and reminds me how lucky I am to be a father,” he wrote in a caption featuring photos of Silas and Phineas. “Watching my sons grow into who they are is life’s greatest gift. Love you two more than you’ll ever know.”

## How We Got Here

This isn’t the first time Biel and Timberlake have shared glimpses into their family life. Biel recently shared a Father’s Day tribute to Timberlake, and he responded with a heartfelt post of his own — both published on June 21. The couple has maintained a balance—sharing just enough to show their bond, but never overexposing their children. That makes Biel’s Comic-Con 2026 comment all the more telling. It wasn’t a staged moment or a curated post. It was a spontaneous, relatable admission from a working mom who wants it all—and wishes physics allowed for pocket-sized kids.

The *Matchbox* premiere on October 9 will likely bring more attention to Biel’s professional life. But for now, the spotlight feels rightly shared—between a wife who admires her husband’s parenting, a mother who misses her children, and a family that, despite fame, seems rooted in the ordinary magic of being together.
