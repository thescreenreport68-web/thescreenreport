## What Was Said

On Sunday (July 19), Jelly Roll’s daughter Bailee Anne DeFord took to her TikTok Live to defend her dad amid rumors of infidelity in his marriage to Bunnie, her step-mother. Nobody else lived in it, so don’t f–king even. I’m for real so done.”

Bailee claimed there’s a reason behind the breakup that would make the internet “crumble,” and she previously said people had no idea what really happened between the couple.

## How We Got Here

Jelly Roll, the “Hands Up” singer, officially filed for divorce from Bunnie Xo in May 2026, citing irreconcilable differences. The couple had been married for 10 years. Their divorce was finalized earlier this month, with settlement details now public.

Bunnie has spoken out about the divorce on her *Dumb Blonde* podcast. An episode diving into the split was deleted despite reportedly earning 100k listens — a move that only deepened the mystery.

The family had once presented a united front. Photos from the 2024 Stagecoach Festival show Jelly Roll, Bailee Ann and Bunnie Xo at the T-Mobile Mane Stage, smiling and relaxed. Bunnie Xo, Jelly Roll, and Bailee Ann DeFord also attended the 66th GRAMMY Awards at Crypto.com Arena, further cementing their image as a blended, supportive unit.

Now, that image is fracturing — not through statements, but through what’s left unsaid.

## The Reaction

This week, Bunnie shared a new video on TikTok and responded to a fan in the comments section who mentioned Bailee’s video. “Nothing to clear up. She didn’t say anything lol ppl just wanna run w a narrative,” Bunnie wrote. She ended the comment with warmth: "Love my baby forever."

Despite Bailee Ann’s fiery hints, she has not released any concrete allegations. She flat out denied that cheating was involved, narrowing the speculation but not eliminating it.

No press statement, no social media post, no interview. The absence of public commentary leaves a vacuum, and Bailee Ann’s words are the loudest thing filling it.

Still, Bunnie’s message is clear: she sees no need to defend herself against a revelation that hasn’t been made.
