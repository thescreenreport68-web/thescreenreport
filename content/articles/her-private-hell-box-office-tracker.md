A Soft Start for a Controversial Vision

This soft start for the sci-fi horror thriller follows a divisive world premiere at the Cannes Film Festival, where the film was met with audience walkouts and what trade reports described as 'befuddled critical notices.' The film's director, Nicolas Winding Refn, has been open about his embrace of controversy, stating that mass appeal is not his goal, which likely contributed to its niche opening performance.

*Her Private Hell* is a 110-minute genre blend of science fiction, horror, and thriller from director Nicolas Winding Refn. The film's premise plunges viewers into a futuristic metropolis engulfed by a mysterious mist that unleashes a deadly, elusive entity. At its core are two desperate quests: a troubled young woman's search for her father collides with an American GI's harrowing odyssey to rescue his daughter from Hell.

## A Star-Studded Cast of Characters

The film features a compelling ensemble led by Sophie Thatcher as Elle, the young woman on her perilous search. Charles Melton stars as Private K, the determined GI. They are joined by Havana Rose Liu as Dominique, Kristine Froseth as Hunter, and Shioli Kutsuna and Aoi Yamada as Ms. T and Ms. S, respectively. Rounding out the principal cast are Dougray Scott as Johnny Thunders and Diego Calva as Nico.
