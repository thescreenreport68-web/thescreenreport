The 1986 animated film *The Transformers: The Movie* is getting a 4K theatrical re-release from Fathom Entertainment in partnership with Hasbro on September 17. According to Deadline, tickets for the global event will go on sale August 7. The re-release will feature an all-new, never-before-seen post-credits sequence, a first for the nearly 40-year-old film.

The announcement was made at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2026, where a new trailer debuted. The event is a cornerstone of Hasbro's "Apology Tour," a year-long campaign acknowledging the pivotal and traumatic death of Optimus Prime in the original movie. The re-release is not just a restoration but an expansion of the classic story.

## What Are the Specifics of the Re-Release?

The event has several key details confirmed by Hasbro and Fathom Entertainment.

* **Release Date:** Theatrical screenings begin on **September 17, 2026** (Transformers Day).
* **Ticket On-Sale Date:** **August 7, 2026**.
* **New Content:** An **exclusive post-credits cinematic chapter** created just for this re-release.
* **Format:** The film has been restored and will be presented in **stunning 4K** and premium large formats like 4DX in select locations.
* **Global Reach:** Screenings are confirmed for the U.S., U.K., Ireland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Mexico, and Germany, with more markets to be announced.
* **Merchandise:** Participating U.S. theaters will offer exclusive collectible concessions, including popcorn buckets and specialty drinkware.

## The Original 1986 Animated Film

Directed by Nelson Shin, *The Transformers: The Movie* is an animated sci-fi adventure that famously escalated the war between the Autobots and Decepticons. The film features a legendary voice cast, including Peter Cullen as Optimus Prime, Frank Welker as Megatron and Soundwave, and the late Orson Welles in his final role as the planet-eating Unicron. Leonard Nimoy voiced the villain Galvatron, and Judd Nelson starred as Hot Rod, who becomes Rodimus Prime.

The plot centers on the Autobots' desperate fight to stop Unicron while also defending the Autobot Matrix of Leadership from an all-out Decepticon assault. Its theatrical release in 1986 was a cultural milestone for fans of the toy line and cartoon, though its dark tone and major character deaths were initially shocking.

## How the Re-Release Fits Hasbro's 'Apology Tour'

The "Apology Tour" is a multifaceted campaign by Hasbro celebrating the film's 40th anniversary while playfully addressing one of its most controversial moments: the death of Optimus Prime. The theatrical re-release is its centerpiece, designed to bring fans back together for a shared experience. The new post-credits scene is a major incentive, offering a fresh narrative hook four decades later.

The tour extends beyond theaters. A reformatted soundtrack album, *The Transformers: The Movie: The Soundtrack: The Reformatted Edition*, is already available on streaming platforms and vinyl. It features re-recorded versions of iconic songs like "The Touch" and "Dare," with artists like Stan Bush and a new take on "Nothin's Gonna Stand in Our Way" by Sebastian Bach. Hasbro has also released a new toy line inspired by the film.

## What's Next for the Theatrical Event?

The next confirmed step is the global ticket sale on August 7. Fans can check the Fathom Entertainment website for participating theater locations and updates on additional international markets. The event is a one-night engagement in many areas, though some theaters may host multiple screenings. Hasbro has indicated the "Apology Tour" will continue throughout the year, but no further theatrical events for this film have been announced.
