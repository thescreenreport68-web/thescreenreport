Michael Johnston leads the cast of the indie horror sensation '[Obsession](/movies/obsession-box-office-tracker/),' a film that has captivated audiences with its chilling premise. The story follows Bear, a hopeless romantic played by Johnston, who breaks the mysterious 'One Wish Willow' to win the heart of his crush. He gets exactly what he asked for, but soon discovers that some desires come at a dark and sinister price. Directed by Curry Barker, this 109-minute horror-thriller explores the terrifying consequences of a wish gone horribly wrong.

## A Talented Ensemble Cast

The film's compelling narrative is brought to life by a dedicated ensemble. Johnston's Bear is supported by Inde Navarrette as Nikki, Cooper Tomlinson as Ian, and Megan Lawless as Sarah. The cast is rounded out by Andy Richter as Carter, Haley Fitzgerald as Viola, Darin Toonder as Harry, and Anthony Pavone as Reggie. Together, they navigate the film's escalating horror, making the terrifying premise feel unsettlingly real.

## At the Box Office

Obsession has grossed $260,344,235 at the domestic box office, 71 days into its theatrical run, while playing across 1,203 theaters. The film added $480,000 in its most recent day of release. Worldwide, it has taken in $448 million. It carries a reported production budget of $750,000.
