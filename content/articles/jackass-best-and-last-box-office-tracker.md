Johnny Knoxville and the original Jackass crew have reunited for one final, chaotic hurrah. The film is the fifth and concluding installment in the long-running franchise, officially billed as *[Jackass: Best and Last](/movies/jackass-best-and-last-box-office-tracker/)*. Under the direction of series co-creator Jeff Tremaine, this 92-minute feature is a genre-blending mix of Action, Comedy, and Documentary that sends the crew on one last insane crusade of outrageous stunts and pranks. It is widely described as a 'greatest hits' compilation, heavily reliant on repurposed footage from the crew's long and painful history.

The full, iconic ensemble is back for this final ride, with every member playing themselves. The cast is led by Johnny Knoxville, alongside Steve-O, Chris Pontius, Jason 'Wee Man' Acuña, Dave England, Preston Lacy, Ehren McGhehey, and Bam Margera. Each brings their own unique brand of fearless, and often foolish, commitment to the film's signature physical comedy. This reunion marks a definitive end to the era-defining series that made a spectacle of self-inflicted harm for laughs.
