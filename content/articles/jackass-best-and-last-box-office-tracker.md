Johnny Knoxville, Steve-O, and the rest of the original Jackass crew have returned for their final, most insane crusade yet. '[Jackass: Best and Last](/movies/jackass-best-and-last-box-office-day-26-adds-38-677-as-domestic-total-hits-18-1/)' is the fifth and conclusive chapter in the landmark franchise, delivering 92 minutes of the unscripted, high-impact stunts and pranks that defined a generation of comedy. Directed by series architect Jeff Tremaine, the film blends action, comedy, and documentary elements into a raucous farewell tour.

The cast sees the full, infamous team reassemble as themselves: Johnny Knoxville, Steve-O, Chris Pontius, Jason 'Wee Man' Acuña, Dave England, Preston Lacy, Ehren McGhehey, and Bam Margera. Each member brings their signature brand of chaotic, self-inflicted humor to the screen for one last round of unforgettable antics. It’s a true ensemble effort, with every participant playing the role they’ve perfected over decades: willing victims in their own spectacularly dumb ideas.
