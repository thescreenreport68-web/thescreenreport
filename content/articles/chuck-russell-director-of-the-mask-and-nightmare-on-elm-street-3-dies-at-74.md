Director Chuck Russell died Wednesday in the San Diego area at the age of 74, according to Variety. His death was confirmed by his attorney.

He also directed 1987's "A Nightmare on Elm Street 3: Dream Warriors," a film credited with revitalizing the horror franchise and launching the career of actress Patricia Arquette.

## What films did Chuck Russell direct?

Russell's filmography as a director includes several notable genre films from the 1980s and 1990s. After making his directorial debut on "A Nightmare on Elm Street 3," he helmed the cult-favorite 1988 remake of "The Blob." Following "The Mask," he directed the Arnold Schwarzenegger action film "Eraser" and "The Scorpion King," which marked Dwayne Johnson's first leading role.

After a hiatus, he returned to direct several later films, including "I Am Wrath" with John Travolta, the Indian film "Junglee," and "Paradise City" with Bruce Willis and Travolta. In 2024, he directed a remake of "Witchboard."

## What did Chuck Russell say about his work?

In a 2024 interview with Variety, Russell spoke about his approach to filmmaking. **"We have to humanize these things, whether it’s action or comedy or drama,"** he said. "You gotta get into your performers’ heads. You gotta get their actual personality into their performances."

Russell also recently announced two productions with his AI company, Neumorphic, which were described as being built with generative AI across their entire production pipeline. He began his career working on screenplays and as a production manager before moving into directing.
