Director Destin Daniel Cretton praised the decision to keep Sadie Sink's role in '[Spider-Man: Brand New Day](/movies/new-spider-man-brand-new-day-trailer-drops-exclusively-in-theaters/)' a closely guarded secret, according to a report from Pajiba. Cretton argued that the secrecy directly benefits the audience's experience.

"Keeping what Sadie’s doing under wraps, you’ll honestly understand when you see the movie," Cretton told The Hollywood Reporter. "The movie is a better movie if you don’t know certain things."

He emphasized that the approach is meant to protect fans, suggesting those who see the film on its opening weekend will have a "much cooler experience" by going in blind. This secrecy has fueled rampant online speculation about who Sink might be playing.

## What is the director's approach to the new film?

Cretton also addressed the challenge of following the previous [Spider-Man](/movies/fans-embrace-a-darker-joking-spider-man-in-brand-new-day-but-some-are-tired-of/) film. He stated that trying to emulate or top that film's scale was not the goal for 'Brand New Day'.

"I don’t think there’s any way of trying to emulate the last movie," Cretton explained. "In terms of stakes, you can’t really go bigger than that last movie. So rather than trying to go bigger, we were hoping to go deeper in a different way."

## How is the film performing ahead of release?

The strategy appears to be generating significant audience interest. Pajiba's report notes that 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' is tracking for an enormous opening weekend, with tickets for early screenings already difficult to secure.
