Prime Video brought the action to San Diego Comic-Con, debuting the official trailer for *Reacher* Season 4 and a first teaser for its upcoming spin-off, *Neagley*. According to TheFutonCritic.com, the panel also revealed the source material for the already-ordered fifth season and a new novel for the franchise. Both series will premiere exclusively on the platform in late summer 2026, marking a major expansion of the hit action universe.

The streamer is building a formidable one-two punch for its fall schedule. Alan Ritchson returns as the formidable drifter Jack Reacher, while Maria Sten steps into the spotlight as his former military protégé turned private investigator. The announcements confirm Prime Video's deep investment in Lee Child's literary world, transforming a single hit show into a budding franchise.

## What Are the Release Dates and Key Details?

The streaming rollout is strategically staggered to keep fans engaged. *Reacher* Season 4 arrives first, with the first three episodes dropping on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. New episodes will then be released weekly. The season finale on September 16, 2026, will serve as a direct lead-in to *Neagley*, which launches all eight of its episodes that same day.

* **Reacher Season 4 Premiere:** August 12, 2026 (3 episodes, then weekly)
* **Neagley Series Premiere:** September 16, 2026 (all 8 episodes)
* **Streaming Platform:** Exclusively on Prime Video
* **Based On:** *Reacher* S4 adapts the 13th novel, *Gone Tomorrow*; *Neagley* is based on the character from Lee Child's novels.
* **Production Studios:** Paramount Television Studios and Amazon MGM Studios

## The Story and Cast of Reacher Season 4

Season 4 plunges Jack Reacher into a high-stakes conspiracy rooted in Lee Child's 13th novel, *Gone Tomorrow*. The official synopsis sets the stage: "When a chance encounter with a distraught stranger on a subway goes horribly wrong, Jack Reacher (Alan Ritchson) is drawn into a complex and deadly game that pits him against ruthless foes from the highest echelons of power." This plot, as detailed by sources like MovieWeb, involves Reacher witnessing a suicide on a New York City subway, an event that pulls him into a dangerous web involving federal agents and Cold War secrets.

Alan Ritchson leads the returning cast, which welcomes several new faces this season. The newcomers include Sydelle Noel, Chris Marquette, AGNEZ MO, Anggun, Kevin Weisman, Marc Blucas, Kevin Corrigan, and Kathleen Robertson. The series is executive produced and showrun by Nick Santora, who created the television adaptation.

## Introducing the Neagley Spin-Off Series

The new series *Neagley* shifts the focus to Frances Neagley, a character familiar to fans of the main show. Maria Sten reprises her role as the sharp, capable private investigator and former member of the Army's 110th Special Investigations Unit. The story finds Neagley in Chicago, determined to uncover the truth behind the suspicious death of a beloved friend from her past.

"Using everything she's learned from Jack Reacher and her time as a member of the 110 Special Investigators, Neagley puts herself on a dangerous path to uncover a menacing evil," states the official series description. The ensemble cast features Greyston Holt as Detective Hudson Riley, Adeline Rudolph as Renee Birdwhistle, Jasper Jones as Keno, Matthew Del Negro as Pierce Woodrow, and Damon Herriman as Lawrence Cole. Alan Ritchson will guest star as Jack Reacher. The series is co-created and executive produced by Nick Santora and Nicholas Wootton.

## How This Expands the Reacher Universe

The dual trailer drop and series announcements signal Prime Video's intent to build a connected action franchise. *Reacher* has been a flagship hit for the streamer, reportedly becoming the first Prime Video original to top Nielsen's overall streaming rankings during its first season. Greenlighting a spin-off before the fourth season even airs demonstrates confidence in the brand's appeal.

The Comic-Con panel added even more long-term fuel, revealing that the already-confirmed Season 5 will adapt the 20th Reacher novel, *Make Me*. Furthermore, Lee Child and author Yasmin Angoe are writing the first-ever *Neagley* novel, *Zero Margin*, scheduled for release in March 2027. This cross-medium expansion mirrors successful franchise playbooks, creating multiple entry points for fans through both television and books.

## What's Next for the Franchise?

With the trailers now public, the next confirmed milestones are the premieres themselves. *Reacher* Season 4 begins its weekly rollout on August 12, 2026, culminating in the full-season drop of *Neagley* on September 16, 2026. Production on Season 5 is underway, ensuring the pipeline of content continues. The announcement of the *Neagley* novel also points to a future where the television and literary branches of the franchise grow in tandem, offering new stories beyond the screen.
