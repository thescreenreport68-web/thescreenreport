The Voyage That Stalled at the Shore

Catherine Laga’ia’s star turn as the titular heroine in Disney's live-action '[Moana](/movies/moana-box-office-tracker/)' was not enough to prevent a shipwreck at the box office. The film's opening weekend was a disaster for the studio, signaling a potential historic low for its lucrative remake strategy.

Directed by Thomas Kail, the 115-minute film is a family fantasy adventure that retells the beloved 2016 animated story. The premise follows teenage Moana as she answers the Ocean's call, voyaging beyond the reef of her island, Motunui, for the first time. She teams up with the infamous, shapeshifting demigod Maui on a quest to restore prosperity to her people, facing mythical creatures and discovering her own destiny along the way.

## A Star-Studded Cast Sails Into Familiar Roles

The film features a blend of new and returning talent. Catherine Laga’ia takes on the lead role of the courageous voyager Moana. Dwayne Johnson reprises his voice role as the charismatic and mighty demigod Maui. The supporting cast includes Rena Owen as the wise Gramma Tala, John Tui as Moana's father, Chief Tui, and Frankie Adams as her mother, Sina. Jemaine Clement returns as the villainous giant crab, Tamatoa, while Emma Puahi-Shapazian and Amaya Masoli also portray younger versions of Moana.

## At the Box Office

Moana has grossed $95,069,653 at the domestic box office, 15 days into its theatrical run, while playing across 4,015 theaters. The film added $3,100,000 in its most recent day of release. Worldwide, it has taken in $191 million. It carries a reported production budget of $250 million.
