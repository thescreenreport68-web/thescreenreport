Catherine Lagaʻaia stars as the title character in Disney's 2026 live-action reimagining of '[Moana](/movies/moana-box-office-tracker/),' directed by Thomas Kail. The film is a 115-minute family, fantasy, comedy, and adventure that follows teenage Moana as she answers the Ocean's call. For the first time, she voyages beyond the reef of her island of Motunui, teaming up with the infamous demigod Maui on an unforgettable journey to restore prosperity to her people.

The cast is led by Catherine Lagaʻaia as the courageous voyager Moana, with Dwayne Johnson returning to voice the charismatic, shapeshifting demigod Maui. They are joined by Rena Owen as the wise Gramma Tala, John Tui as Chief Tui, Frankie Adams as Sina, and Jemaine Clement as the villainous giant crab Tamatoa. Emma Puahi-Shapazian and Amaya Masoli are also credited in the role of Moana. It is a fresh cinematic voyage bringing the beloved animated tale to a new generation.
