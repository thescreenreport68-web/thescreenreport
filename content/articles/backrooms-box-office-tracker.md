Chiwetel Ejiofor leads a chilling ensemble in *[Backrooms](/movies/backrooms-box-office-day-52-adds-222-066-as-domestic-total-hits-196-million/)*, a horror-mystery that has unlocked a staggering new financial dimension for indie powerhouse A24.

Directed by Kane Parsons, *Backrooms* is a 111-minute descent into sci-fi horror. The premise is deceptively simple yet profoundly unsettling: a strange doorway appears in the basement of a furniture showroom. This gateway leads not to another room, but to a seemingly infinite, liminal space of yellowed wallpaper and fluorescent hum—the infamous "Backrooms" of internet legend. The film explores the psychological and physical terror of those who discover and become trapped within this non-Euclidean nightmare.

The cast is led by Oscar nominee Chiwetel Ejiofor as Clark, a man grappling with the reality-warping discovery. He is joined by Renate Reinsve (*The Worst Person in the World*) as Mary, with Ember Ambrose portraying a younger version of the character. Finn Bennett plays Bobby, while Lukita Maxwell appears as Kat. The supporting cast includes Mark Duplass as Phil, Avan Jogia as Naren Warne, and Robert Bobroczkyi as Pirate Clark, rounding out a group of characters drawn into the inexplicable phenomenon.

The film's financial performance is nothing short of historic.

## At the Box Office

Backrooms has grossed $196,437,365 at the domestic box office, 56 days into its theatrical run, while playing across 742 theaters. The film added $101,465 in its most recent day of release. Worldwide, it has taken in $388 million. It carries a reported production budget of $10 million.
