The film marks the feature directorial debut of Kane Parsons, who crafts a 111-minute tale of cosmic dread.

The premise is deceptively simple yet deeply unsettling: a strange, inexplicable doorway materializes in the basement of a mundane furniture showroom. This portal becomes the focal point for a genre-bending story that blends horror, mystery, and science fiction, plunging its characters into an uncanny and terrifying reality.

The ensemble cast brings this eerie story to life. Chiwetel Ejiofor stars as Clark, the central figure drawn into the mystery. He is joined by Renate Reinsve as Mary, Finn Bennett as Bobby, and Lukita Maxwell as Kat. Mark Duplass appears as Phil, with Avan Jogia playing Naren Warne. Robert Bobroczkyi and Ember Ambrose round out the cast as Pirate Clark and Young Mary, respectively. Together, they navigate the film's haunting and unpredictable narrative. The result is a uniquely atmospheric film that has captured the audience's imagination.
