The Epic's Journey to the Screen

Matt Damon anchors a legendary cast in Christopher Nolan's ambitious cinematic voyage, *[The Odyssey](/movies/the-odyssey-box-office-tracker/)*. This sweeping adaptation brings Homer's ancient epic to life with a modern blockbuster scale, promising a visual and narrative spectacle.

The film follows Odysseus, the legendary King of Ithaca, on his long and perilous journey home following the Trojan War. Throughout his voyage, he is forced to confront the whims of gods, mythological monsters, and trials that stretch both his cunning and his humanity to the breaking point. Directed by Christopher Nolan, this 173-minute adventure, action, and fantasy epic is the first feature ever shot entirely with Imax cameras, designed for a monumental theatrical experience.

## A Star-Studded Mythological Crew

The ensemble is led by Matt Damon as the cunning hero, Odysseus. He is joined by Tom Holland as his son, Telemachus, and Anne Hathaway as his faithful wife, Penelope. Robert Pattinson plays the antagonistic suitor Antinous, while Himesh Patel portrays Odysseus's loyal companion Eurylochus. The cast is rounded out by John Leguizamo as the swineherd Eumaeus, Charlize Theron as the enchanting nymph Calypso, and a cameo from Travis Scott as a Bard. It is a grand reunion of Nolan's frequent collaborators and new faces, bringing the ancient world to vivid life.
