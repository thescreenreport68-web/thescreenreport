Matt Damon’s ‘The Odyssey’ Sails Into Cinemas

Christopher Nolan’s latest cinematic venture, ‘[The Odyssey](/movies/the-odyssey-box-office-tracker/),’ brings the ancient Greek epic to life with a stellar cast led by Matt Damon. The film plunges audiences into a world of gods, monsters, and heroic endurance.

The film is a grand-scale adventure, action, and fantasy epic with a runtime of 173 minutes. Directed by Christopher Nolan, it follows the legendary King of Ithaca, Odysseus, on his long and perilous voyage home after the Trojan War. His journey is a relentless trial, forcing him to confront the whims of vengeful gods, terrifying mythological beasts, and challenges that test his legendary cunning and his very humanity to its breaking point.

## A Stellar Cast Brings the Myth to Life

Matt Damon carries the film as the weary but resilient hero, Odysseus. He is supported by Tom Holland as his son, Telemachus, and Anne Hathaway as his faithful wife, Penelope. Robert Pattinson plays the antagonistic suitor Antinous, while Himesh Patel appears as Odysseus’s loyal companion Eurylochus. The cast is rounded out by John Leguizamo as the swineherd Eumaeus, Charlize Theron as the enchanting nymph Calypso, and a cameo from Travis Scott as a Bard.

The film has been met with critical acclaim, described in industry reporting as one of the best-reviewed films of the year. This strong reception has fueled significant audience interest, helping it debut at No. Christopher Nolan has once again crafted a spectacle that resonates with both critics and moviegoers, marking another major achievement in his filmography.

## The Box Office Voyage of a Lifetime

‘The Odyssey’ has made a monumental splash at the box office. Its performance includes the second-best Wednesday for director Christopher Nolan at the domestic box office, trailing only ‘The Dark Knight’ from 2008. It stands as Nolan’s biggest debut since 2012’s ‘The Dark Knight Rises’ and is the industry’s third-largest opening of the year.

Most impressively, the film’s global launch is now the biggest of Nolan’s career.

## At the Box Office

The Odyssey has grossed $199,371,800 at the domestic box office, 7 days into its theatrical run, while playing across 3,919 theaters. The film added $17,625,485 in its most recent day of release. Worldwide, it has taken in $322 million. It carries a reported production budget of $250 million.
