The animated sequel film *Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender* is now streaming on Paramount+. This release marks the first official project from Avatar Studios and revives the beloved franchise with a story set over a decade after the original Nickelodeon series.

According to Variety, the film was originally slated for a theatrical release but is now available primarily on the streaming service, with only a limited, awards-qualifying run in New York and Los Angeles theaters. This shift means most fans will experience the film's much-praised visuals on the small screen.

## What Are the Key Details of the Film?

* **Streaming Platform:** Paramount+ (as of July 2026)
* **Release Date:** July 24, 2026
* **Director:** Lauren Montgomery, a veteran of the *Avatar* and *Korra* animated series.
* **Runtime:** 99 minutes (rated PG).
* **Story Setting:** Over a decade after the events of the original *Avatar: The Last Airbender* series.
* **Central Plot:** Adult Aang discovers a second Airbender, Tagah, preserved in ice, and races to recover a sacred artifact before a cult of non-benders called the Denied can use it.

## Who Voices the Characters?

The film features a mix of new talent and actors stepping into iconic roles. Eric Nam voices the adult Aang, taking over from the original child actor. Steven Yeun voices the adult Fire Lord Zuko. Wrestler-turned-actor Dave Bautista joins the cast as Tagah, the newly discovered Airbender.

The core Team Avatar is also back, voiced by a new ensemble: Jessica Matten as Katara, Román Zaragoza as Sokka, and Dionne Quan as Toph. Dee Bradley Baker returns to voice the beloved animal companions Appa and Momo. The supporting cast includes Freida Pinto as a past Avatar named Sonam and Taika Waititi as a spirit-world creature.

## How Does the Film Expand the Avatar World?

Directed by Lauren Montgomery and written by Tim Hedrick and Christopher Yost, the film explores a more mature Aang grappling with the loneliness of being the last of his culture. The A.V. Club notes the film operates as a "gorgeous and breezy reunion special" that presumes familiarity with the characters, focusing on Aang's brief but intense relationship with Tagah.

The narrative delves into the long-term trauma of Aang's loss, a theme that resonates with the original fanbase who have aged alongside the character. The film is set during a time of fragile peace, with the newly established Republic City serving as a backdrop. Series creators Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko, who serve as co-chief creative officers of Avatar Studios, share story credit on the project.

## What Is the Critical Reception?

Reviews highlight the film's primary strength: its animation and action sequences.

However, some critics point to narrative limitations. The A.V. The outlet concludes the story feels "inconsequential" and that the premise might have been better served as a season of television.

## What's Next for the Avatar Franchise?

*Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender* ends the franchise's dozen-year dry spell since *The Legend of Korra* concluded in 2014. Its release on Paramount+ is the first step in Avatar Studios' plan to expand the universe. A new, original animated series co-created by DiMartino and Konietzko, titled *Seven Havens*, is confirmed to premiere on Paramount+ later in 2026.

## The Casting of Ke Huy Quan

This marks a significant addition to the ensemble, bringing an acclaimed performer into the franchise's spiritual lineage.

Quan's role as Avatar Xian represents another layer of the film's exploration of Air Nomad history and cultural identity. His casting, alongside other notable actors, underscores the project's appeal to a generation of performers who are now fans of the original series.
