AMC has renewed its acclaimed series *[Interview With the Vampire](/tv/interview-with-the-vampire-renewed-for-season-4-titled-queen-of-the-damned/)* for a fourth season, which will be rebranded as *Queen of the Damned*.

The renewal continues the show's unique tradition of adopting the title of the Anne Rice novel it adapts each season. Season four will be based on the third book in Rice's *Vampire Chronicles* series, following the recently concluded *[The Vampire Lestat](/tv/the-vampire-lestat-finale-sent-fans-into-a-frenzy/)*. The new installment will shift its narrative focus to the ancient and powerful first vampire, Akasha, portrayed by Sheila Atim.

## What Are the Key Details for Season 4?

The confirmed specifics for the upcoming season include:
* **New Title:** *Anne Rice's Queen of the Damned*
* **New Showrunner:** Hannah Moscovitch
* **Central Character:** Akasha (Sheila Atim)
* **Source Material:** Anne Rice's 1988 novel *Queen of the Damned*
* **Executive Producers:** Mark Johnson, Rolin Jones, and Hannah Moscovitch

Hannah Moscovitch, who wrote for the first two seasons and served as an executive producer on *The Vampire Lestat*, will take over showrunning duties from series creator Rolin Jones. Jones will remain with the series as an executive producer. Moscovitch has a deep history with the character of Akasha, having specifically written for her and the character Gabriella (Jennifer Ehle) in the previous season.

## Who Is Behind the Series?

The series, which first aired in October 2022, was created by Rolin Jones. It stars Jacob Anderson as Louis de Pointe du Lac and Sam Reid as Lestat de Lioncourt, with a supporting cast including Assad Zaman, Eric Bogosian, Delainey Hayles, and Ben Daniels. The show reimagines Anne Rice's gothic horror saga, following a century-old vampire recounting his life of immortality, bloodlust, and a tumultuous relationship with the rakish Frenchman who turned him.

The series is currently available to stream on multiple platforms in the United States, including Netflix, AMC+, and YouTube TV.

## Why Does This Renewal and Rebrand Matter?

The decision to renew and rename the series for a second consecutive season is a bold creative bet for AMC. It signals a commitment to fully adapting Rice's expansive literary universe, book by book, rather than keeping a single series title. This approach allows each season to have a distinct identity while maintaining narrative continuity. The shift in showrunner from Jones to Moscovitch also represents a planned creative handoff, ensuring the writer most familiar with the new season's central antagonist is guiding the story.

In a statement, Dan McDermott, Chief Content Officer of AMC Global Media and President of AMC Studios, praised the team behind *The Vampire Lestat* and set the stakes for the next chapter. "We can’t wait to continue the journey with *Queen of the Damned*, one of the most iconic stories in Anne Rice’s mythology," McDermott said, "and watch our established and beloved characters confront Akasha with their world, and ours, very much hanging in the balance."

## What Happens Next for Queen of the Damned?

As of July 2026, AMC has not announced a production start date or a premiere window for *Queen of the Damned*. The network has released a brief teaser for the season, which prominently features Sheila Atim's Akasha, to build anticipation following the cliffhanger ending of *The Vampire Lestat*. Fans can expect the story to pick up directly from that finale, exploring the apocalyptic consequences of Akasha's awakening as she seeks to rule the world.

## The Season Three Finale Cliffhanger

The renewal announcement follows the recent conclusion of the third season, *The Vampire Lestat*, which left its audience on a significant cliffhanger. The finale glimpsed the destructive onset of Akasha's reign, setting the stage for the next chapter. Series creator Rolin Jones hinted at this impending shift in a finale interview, stating, "If you didn’t like this season or you cuddled up to it and you loved it desperately, get ready. We’re going to do it again. It’s going to flip."

Sheila Atim's Akasha first appeared in Episode 5 of *The Vampire Lestat*, establishing her character before the season's climactic ending. The new season will directly continue from that point, exploring the apocalyptic consequences of her awakening.
