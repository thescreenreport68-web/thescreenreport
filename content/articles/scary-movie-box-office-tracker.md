Anna Faris, Regina Hall, and the Wayans brothers are back in the killer's crosshairs. *[Scary Movie](/movies/scary-movie-box-office-day-47-adds-34-691-as-domestic-total-hits-108-million/) (2026)* marks the return of the franchise's original 'Core Four' for a new round of R-rated, meta-horror mayhem.

Directed by Michael Tiddes, the 96-minute comedy-horror film finds Cindy, Brenda, Shorty, and Ray pulled back into danger twenty-six years after their first encounter. This time, no contemporary horror movie intellectual property is safe from the film's signature brand of parody. The premise follows the veteran characters as they once again try to outrun a suspiciously familiar masked killer.

The ensemble cast is a mix of beloved veterans and new faces. Anna Faris reprises her role as the perpetually imperiled Cindy Campbell, with Regina Hall returning as the sharp-tongued Brenda Meeks. Marlon Wayans is back in multiple hilarious disguises, playing Shorty, 'Joe', 'Tiffany', and even Count Brolock. Shawn Wayans returns as Ray. They are joined by newcomers Olivia Rose Keegan as Sara, Savannah Lee Nassif as Tuesday, Cameron Scott Roberts as Jack, and Sydney Park as DEI. The film is a celebration of the series' chaotic spirit, proving the core cast's chemistry remains as potent as ever.
