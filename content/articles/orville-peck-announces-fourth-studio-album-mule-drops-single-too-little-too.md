Orville Peck has announced his fourth studio album, 'Mule,' and released its lead single.

The album is described as a chronological timeline of a year that reshaped his life, tracing the collapse of addiction, the pursuit of sobriety, and the process of rebuilding. Peck previewed the LP with two singles: the meditative, 48-second a cappella hymn "Prologue (Eulogy of A Man Turned Mule)," featuring Emmylou Harris, Noah Cyrus, and Allison Russell, and the rollicking pop-country jam "Too Little, Too Late."

## What is the new single about?

"Too Little, Too Late" is a co-write between Peck, Steve Rusch, and Semisonic's Dan Wilson. The track is described as a sweet and sassy kiss-off to a lover who tries to come back. Peck sings over hand-clap drums and a finger-snapping beat, with lyrics including, "You want me hangin' round but you hate when I complain / You're back again, you wanna go / You, love songs on your radio / Those records are all burned out anyway."

## When is the tour and how do I get tickets?

The singer will donate $1 from every ticket sold to The Trevor Project to support its crisis intervention and suicide prevention services for LGBTQ+ young people. An artist presale is scheduled to begin on July 28 at 10 a.m. local time, with a public on-sale following on July 31.

## What is the tracklist for 'Mule'?

The new album contains 13 songs. The full tracklist runs from "Prologue (Eulogy of a Man Turned Mule)" through to the closing title track, "Mule."
