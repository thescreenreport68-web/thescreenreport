Hulu has greenlit three extended-length episodes of *Futurama*, according to Deadline, with the first Christmas-themed special expected to launch in 2027. The announcement came during the series' panel at San Diego Comic-Con on Saturday, where a new trailer for the upcoming 14th season also debuted. The long-running animated sci-fi sitcom will return with new episodes on Monday, August 3.

The show, created by Matt Groening, has been a staple of adult animation since its premiere in 1999. It follows the adventures of pizza delivery boy Philip J. Fry, who is cryogenically frozen and wakes up in the 31st century to work at the interplanetary delivery company Planet Express. The series has won six Primetime Emmy Awards and is highly rated by audiences.

## What Are the Key Details for Season 14 and the Specials?

The news confirms a significant expansion of the *Futurama* universe on Hulu. The key developments are:

- **Three Extended Specials:** Hulu has ordered three "XXXL-sized" *Futurama* episodes, currently in production
- **First Special Theme:** The first of these specials will be Christmas-themed and is expected to launch in 2027
- **Season 14 Premiere:** The 14th season premieres on **Monday, August 3**, with two new episodes
- **Streaming Platforms:** New episodes stream on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+ for bundle subscribers, and on Disney+ internationally
- **Core Cast Returns:** Season 14 stars the main voice cast: Billy West (Fry, Professor Farnsworth), Katey Sagal (Leela), John DiMaggio (Bender), Tress MacNeille (Mom), Maurice LaMarche (Morbo, Kif), Lauren Tom (Amy), Phil LaMarr (Hermes), and David Herman (Scruffy)

Details about the other two specials are being kept under wraps for now. The new trailer for Season 14, shown at Comic-Con, promises an "intergalactic and perhaps inter-dimensional mission." Its official logline teases "a non-stop onslaught of swaggering space pirates, lab-grown meats, sexy scams… plus the shocking return of Dr. Zoidberg’s lost love!"

## Who Is Behind the Return of Futurama?

*Futurama* is created by Matt Groening and was developed by Groening and David X. Cohen. The series is executive produced by Groening, Cohen, Ken Keeler, and Claudia Katz. It hails from 20th Television Animation, a part of Disney Television Studios, with animation provided by Rough Draft Studios.

The show has been revived multiple times in different formats, including as a series of direct-to-DVD movies and subsequent seasons on Comedy Central. The series is celebrated for its blend of sharp social satire, science fiction parody, and adult humor, exploring themes from space travel and alien worlds to melancholy and speculative futures.

## How Does This Fit Into Futurama's Legacy?

The greenlight for three extended specials signals Hulu's strong continued investment in the property beyond the standard season order. This move echoes the show's history of returning in different formats. The commitment to "XXXL-sized" episodes suggests a confidence in the creative team's ability to handle longer-form storytelling within the *Futurama* universe, a challenge the writers have navigated before.

The show's enduring popularity is evidenced by its critical acclaim and fan loyalty. According to authoritative data from OMDb, the series has garnered 30 wins and 59 nominations total, including its six Primetime Emmy Awards. Its cult status makes it a valuable franchise for Hulu, especially as part of the Disney-owned streaming portfolio.

## What's Next for Futurama?

The immediate next step is the premiere of Season 14 on August 3. New episodes will continue to stream on Mondays on Hulu. Production on the three extended specials is underway, with the Christmas-themed episode targeted for a 2027 release. While plot details for the specials remain secret, the show's producers have a history of revisiting and reworking beloved ideas; writer Ken Keeler has previously expressed a desire to reuse a cut gag involving Bender in a dress from an earlier movie.

For now, fans can look forward to a new season of the Planet Express crew's adventures this summer, with the promise of even more oversized content on the horizon.
