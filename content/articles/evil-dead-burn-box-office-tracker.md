Souheila Yacoub Confronts a Demonic Family Legacy in 'Evil Dead Burn'

Souheila Yacoub leads the cast of *[Evil Dead Burn](/movies/evil-dead-burn-box-office-tracker/)*, the latest chapter in the long-running horror saga. The film marks a new direction for the franchise under the direction of Sébastien Vaniček.

The 110-minute horror film follows Alice (Yacoub), who seeks comfort with her late husband's remaining family after his sudden death. She soon discovers her in-laws are descendants of a leading researcher on demonic possession. As family members begin to transform into creatures that feed on fear, Alice learns that the vows she took in life must now be tested against forces from beyond the grave.

The ensemble cast is led by Souheila Yacoub as the grieving widow, Alice. She is joined by Tandi Wright as Susan, Hunter Doohan as Joseph, and Luciane Buchanan as Thya, members of the afflicted family tree. Erroll Shand portrays Edgar, with Maude Davey as Polly, George Pullar as Will, and Victory Ndukwe rounding out the cast as Leo. Together, they face a terrifying legacy.

The *Evil Dead* series maintains a unique distinction: all six films, including this new entry, have received positive scores from critics on Rotten Tomatoes. This consistent reception underscores the franchise's enduring appeal and its ability to reinvent itself for new audiences. *Evil Dead Burn* continues a legacy of cult horror that has terrified fans for decades.
