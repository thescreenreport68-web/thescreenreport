Director Adam Wingard confirmed his upcoming film 'Onslaught' is set in the same universe as his cult hit 'The Guest,' according to a report from IMDb.

Wingard revealed the connection, describing the new film as part of a secret trilogy with 'The Guest.' The news broke alongside the reveal of new IMAX posters for the project.

## What is the connection to 'The Guest'?

The director's confirmation directly links the two films under a single narrative umbrella. While specific plot ties were not detailed, the announcement positions 'Onslaught' as a continuation of the world established in the earlier thriller. This revelation recontextualizes 'The Guest' as the first chapter in a larger, previously unannounced story.

## What do we know about 'Onslaught'?

The film is an action-horror thriller starring Adria Arjona as Celeste, a military sniper forced to unleash hell to protect her young daughter. Her mission is to stop a rogue squad of genetically engineered super soldiers who have broken loose in the desert. The cast also includes Alex Pereira, Drew Starkey, Rebecca Hall, and Dan Stevens.

## When does 'Onslaught' come out?

The newly released IMAX posters offer a first look at the film's intense, gritty aesthetic ahead of its debut next year.

This developing story, as reported by IMDb, provides a major new piece of lore for fans of Wingard's work, connecting two of his projects for the first time.
