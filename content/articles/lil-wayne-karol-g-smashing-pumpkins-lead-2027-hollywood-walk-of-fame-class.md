[Lil Wayne](/celebrity/lil-wayne-apparently-shows-up-2-hours-late-to-concert-in-new-hampshire/), Karol G, and the Smashing Pumpkins are among the 32 entertainment figures selected to receive stars on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2027, according to an announcement from the Walk of Fame Selection Committee. The new class spans motion pictures, television, recording, live performance, radio, and sports entertainment, marking a diverse group of artists and executives.

The official list was unveiled at a July 23, 2026, press conference at Funko Hollywood by Hollywood Chamber of Commerce President Ron Frierson and Walk of Famers Ming-Na Wen and Sheila E. The selections were ratified by the Chamber’s Board of Directors after a nomination meeting in May 2026.

## Who Else Is Getting a Star in 2027?

The full Class of 2027, as detailed on the official Hollywood Walk of Fame website, breaks down by category.

* **Motion Pictures:** Cheech & Chong, Idris Elba, Sam Elliott, Elle Fanning & Dakota Fanning (double ceremony), Kate Hudson, Delroy Lindo, Sam Rockwell, Ted Sarandos.
* **Television:** David Alan Grier, Lisa Kudrow, Bill Lawrence, Pedro Pascal, Adam Scott, Jeff Probst, Keke Palmer, Raven-Symoné.
* **Recording:** [Karol G](/music/karol-g-kicks-off-viajando-por-el-mundo-tropitour-in-chicago/), David Guetta, Waylon Jennings (Posthumous), The Ramones, Grandmaster Flash, Marc Shaiman, Sia, Smashing Pumpkins, Linkin Park, Lil Wayne.
* **Live Theatre/Live Performance:** Jo Koy, Nicole Scherzinger, Roberto Bolle.
* **Sports Entertainment:** Jimmie Johnson.
* **Radio:** Charlamagne tha God.

This class represents a significant cross-section of modern entertainment, from global music stars to beloved television actors and industry executives like Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos.

## Notable Firsts and Historic Honors

Several selections carry special significance. Elle and Dakota Fanning will become the first sister duo to receive a joint star ceremony on the Walk of Fame. The Recording category honors both living legends and posthumous recognition, with country icon Waylon Jennings and foundational hip-hop artist Grandmaster Flash set to be commemorated.

Italian ballet dancer Roberto Bolle, one of the honorees in the Live Performance category, shared his reaction to the news. "There are milestones that seem like they belong in dreams – until the day they become reality," Bolle said. "Joining the Hollywood Walk of Fame Class of 2027 is one of those moments."

## What the Committee Said About the Class

In a statement, Walk of Fame Selection Chairman Peter Roth framed the honor as a celebration of lasting cultural impact. "The Walk of Fame Selection Committee is proud to welcome the Class of 2027 to one of the most enduring symbols of achievement in entertainment," Roth said. "These 32 extraordinary individuals have each made a lasting impact on audiences around the world through their talent, creativity, and dedication to their craft."

Ron Frierson echoed the sentiment during the press conference, stating, "This year's honorees reflect the breadth, diversity and imagination of the entertainment community: Performers whose voices moved us, storytellers who expanded our view of the world, visionaries who brought unforgettable characters to life and trailblazers whose influence will be felt for generations to come."

## When Will the Star Ceremonies Happen?

As of July 2026, no specific ceremony dates have been scheduled for the Class of 2027. According to the official Walk of Fame announcement, recipients have up to two years from the date of selection to schedule their star ceremonies before the accolade expires. The Hollywood Chamber of Commerce typically announces upcoming ceremonies about ten days in advance on its official website.

## Smashing Pumpkins and Ramones' Upcoming Milestones

While awaiting their star ceremonies, some honorees have other significant events on the horizon. According to Consequence, the Smashing Pumpkins are set to embark on a fall North American tour celebrating the 30th anniversary of their album *Mellon Collie and the Infinite Sadness*. These milestones highlight the enduring legacy of the artists selected for the Walk of Fame's Class of 2027.
