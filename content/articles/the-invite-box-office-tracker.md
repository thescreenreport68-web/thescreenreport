The film, directed by Olivia Wilde, runs for 107 minutes and blends drama with comedy. The premise centers on Joe and Angela, a married couple whose relationship is on thin ice. In a bid to smooth things over or perhaps out of sheer desperation, they invite their enigmatic upstairs neighbors over for a dinner party. What begins as an awkward social gathering quickly spirals into unexpected and unhinged territory, testing the limits of their hospitality and their marriage.

The ensemble cast is led by Wilde herself, who stars as Angela, the wife at the center of the crumbling marriage. Seth Rogen plays her husband, Joe. Their mysterious and provocative new neighbors are portrayed by two powerhouse actors: Penélope Cruz plays Pina, and Edward Norton takes on the role of Hawk. The supporting cast includes Skip Howland as a Doorman, Mel Powell as a Bartender, and Rachel Thurow and Mario Valdez as Subway Goers, rounding out the world of this increasingly chaotic night.
