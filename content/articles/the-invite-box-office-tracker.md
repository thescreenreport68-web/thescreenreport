A Tense Dinner Party for Four

Olivia Wilde directs and stars in the comedic drama *[The Invite](/movies/the-invite-box-office-day-24-adds-1-2-million-as-domestic-total-hits-15-million/)*, which turns a simple social gathering into a pressure cooker for a faltering marriage. The film, with a runtime of 107 minutes, follows Joe and Angela, a couple whose relationship is on thin ice. Their attempt to salvage things by inviting their mysterious upstairs neighbors for dinner spirals the night into unexpected and revealing territory.

## A Stellar Ensemble Cast

Wilde stars as Angela, while Seth Rogen plays her husband, Joe. Their enigmatic new neighbors are brought to life by Penélope Cruz as Pina and Edward Norton as Hawk. The supporting cast includes Skip Howland as a Doorman, Mel Powell as a Bartender, and Rachel Thurow and Mario Valdez as Subway Goers, rounding out the world of this intimate, character-driven story.

## The Invite box office weekend drop

The film's premise of social unease and marital strife, delivered by a top-tier cast, aims to connect with audiences looking for a blend of sharp comedy and dramatic tension.
