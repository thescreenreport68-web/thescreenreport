Marvel Comics announced a new five-issue Indiana Jones limited series, titled *Indiana Jones and the Sword of Pandemonium*, at the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel during San Diego Comic-Con, according to [Marvel](/movies/avengers-doomsday-trailer-sent-fans-into-a-frenzy-and-the-skeptics-came-out/).com. The series will launch a new era of comic book adventures for the archaeologist.

The story is set in the wake of *Indiana Jones and the Raiders of the Lost Ark*. The plot sees Indy's most notorious villains seeking a terrifying new source of power, which has fallen into the hands of his former companion, Marion Ravenwood. Indiana Jones must then race across the globe in search of a biblical weapon, testing both his archaeology skills and his skepticism of the supernatural.

## Who is creating the new Indiana Jones comic?

The series is written by Jason Aaron and drawn by Aaron Kuder. Aaron expressed his enthusiasm, noting he'd wanted to adventure alongside Indiana Jones since childhood and compared the feeling to launching *[Star Wars](/movies/a-star-wars-powerwash-pack-has-fans-in-a-chokehold-but-not-for-the-reason-you/) #1* in 2015. Kuder added, "I’m very excited to be on this book with Jason Aaron. Indie has always felt like he should be a staple of a comic book character."

## When does the first issue come out?

The announcement follows recent news of deluxe hardcover collections of the character's 1980s Marvel Comics stories, which are set for release in September.
