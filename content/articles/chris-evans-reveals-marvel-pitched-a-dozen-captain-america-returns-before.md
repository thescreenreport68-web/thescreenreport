Chris Evans revealed that Marvel Studios pitched him at least a dozen different ways to return as Captain America before he agreed to come back for [Avengers: Doomsday](/movies/avengers-doomsday-trailer-drops-with-fans-cheering-robert-downey-jr-s/). The actor made the comments in an interview with Entertainment Weekly at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2026, explaining his long road back to the role.

Evans last played Steve Rogers in 2019's Avengers: Endgame, which gave the character a definitive, fan-favorite conclusion by sending him back in time to live a life with Peggy Carter. Despite that closure, Marvel producers [Kevin Feige](/movies/adam-driver-mcu-tradition-kevin-feige-laughs-off-actor-s-habit-of-turning/) and Lou D'Esposito began discussing a return almost immediately.

## What Pitches Did Evans Turn Down?

According to Evans, the first serious pitch came right after Endgame wrapped in 2018. "When we finished ‘Endgame’ in 2018, Lou and Kevin took me to dinner and they were like, ‘We’d love to keep doing movies with Steve Rogers as Nomad,’" Evans told EW. Nomad is an alternate identity Steve Rogers adopted in the comics after abandoning the Captain America mantle.

The actor said the pitch was great, but it didn't feel right at the time. "I was a little precious. I wanted to preserve it," he admitted. He asked the executives to keep trying, and they did—for years.

* At least a dozen separate pitches for Captain America's return over eight years.
* An initial concept featuring Steve Rogers as Nomad.
* A final decision triggered by Robert Downey Jr.'s involvement and director Joe Russo's vision.
* A theatrical release date of December 18, 2026.

"Over the course of eight years, I probably heard at least a dozen pitches," Evans said. "And they were all wonderful. It just never quite felt right."

## What Finally Convinced Him to Return?

The turning point came with news about his former co-star. Evans said he got a call from his team about a rumor that Robert Downey Jr. was in talks to rejoin the [Marvel](/movies/avengers-doomsday-fans-are-hyped-critics-are-skeptical/) Cinematic Universe. "I was like, ‘Uh, oh. What does this mean?’" Evans recalled. "And all of a sudden it felt right."

Director Joe Russo then presented the pitch for Avengers: Doomsday with Downey attached. "It just all of a sudden… it was like, ‘This is it,’" Evans said.

Downey is not returning as Tony Stark/Iron Man, who died in Endgame. Instead, he is playing the iconic villain Victor von Doom, also known as Doctor Doom. Evans will reprise his role as Steve Rogers, though the narrative circumstances of his return are being kept secret.

## What Is Avengers: Doomsday?

The film is a major crossover event directed by Joe Russo. According to its official logline, beloved heroes from three distinct universes are set on a deadly collision course against an existential threat. The confirmed cast showcases the scale of the project, blending original Avengers with new characters from the Fantastic Four.

* **Robert Downey Jr.** as Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom
* **Chris Evans** as Steve Rogers
* **Chris Hemsworth** as Thor
* **Pedro Pascal** as Reed Richards / Mister Fantastic
* **Paul Rudd** as Scott Lang / Ant-Man
* **Anthony Mackie** as Sam Wilson / Captain America
* **Florence Pugh** as Yelena Belova
* **Vanessa Kirby** as Sue Storm / Invisible Woman
* **Ebon Moss-Bachrach** as Ben Grimm / The Thing
* **Wyatt Russell** as John Walker

The film is a theatrical release with a runtime of 165 minutes, blending science fiction, action, and adventure.

## Why Does This Return Matter?

Evans's return, after so many rejected ideas, signals the immense stakes of Avengers: Doomsday. Bringing back one of the core original Avengers, alongside Downey in a completely new role, is a major bet for Marvel. It suggests the story requires the emotional weight and history these actors bring, even as the narrative pushes into new multiversal territory.

The actor's candid explanation also highlights the careful consideration given to legacy characters. His desire to "preserve" Steve Rogers' ending shows a reluctance to undermine a beloved arc for a mere cameo, raising expectations that his role in Doomsday will be substantive and meaningful.

## What's Next for Avengers: Doomsday?

The film is scheduled for release in theaters on December 18, 2026. Evans and Downey appeared together at San Diego Comic-Con to promote the project, marking a public reunion for the two stars. As of July 2026, specific plot details surrounding Captain America's return remain under wraps.
