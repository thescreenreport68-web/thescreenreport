The official exhibition "[Star Wars](/movies/a-star-wars-powerwash-pack-has-fans-in-a-chokehold-but-not-for-the-reason-you/): The Experience" will world premiere at The Franklin Institute in Philadelphia on February 13, 2027, according to PR Newswire. This marks the official exhibition for the global celebration of the franchise's 50th anniversary.

Developed by The Franklin Institute in collaboration with Lucasfilm, Disney Consumer Products, and MDSX, the exhibition is described as an epic journey through the Star Wars legacy. It aims to take fans inside the galaxy's creation, celebrating the art, innovation, and fandom that have defined the saga.

## What will the exhibition include?

The all-new exhibition will trace the legacy of Star Wars from its handcrafted beginnings to today's digital frontiers. It will explore sound design, creature creation, fan art, and collectibles.

Visitors will see a rare collection of iconic, screen-used artifacts, many on public display for the first time. Highlights include Darth Maul's lightsaber and Darth Vader's costume.

## What did organizers say about the project?

"Debuting the world premiere of Star Wars™: The Experience in Philadelphia is a tremendous honor for The Franklin Institute and for our city," said Larry Dubinski, President and CEO of The Franklin Institute. He noted the franchise's inspiration through imagination and innovation.

Lynwen Brennan, Co-President of Lucasfilm, emphasized the collaborative spirit. "Lucasfilm has always been at the forefront of merging cutting edge technology and limitless storytelling," Brennan said. "We are creating an experience that reflects that very spirit of curiosity and creativity."

## What unique experiences are planned?

Newly released details from San Diego [Comic-Con](/movies/san-diego-comic-con-2026-a-celebration-of-comics-or-a-digital-pop-culture/) outline several immersive zones. Guests can walk through a re-creation of Queen Amidala's Throne Room, encounter a full-scale Han Solo in carbonite prop for photo opportunities, and explore a "Wireframe Worlds" gallery anchored by a wireframe TIE fighter.

The exhibition will conclude with a display of iconic Star Wars action figures from the past 50 years.

First exhibit renderings and official key art were unveiled at San Diego Comic-Con during a panel moderated by Ashley Eckstein, the voice of Ahsoka Tano.
