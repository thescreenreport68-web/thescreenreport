Prime Video transported fans back to Middle-earth at San Diego Comic-Con, unveiling a new teaser trailer and major casting news for the third season of *The Lord of the Rings: The [Rings of Power](/tv/jamie-campbell-bower-joins-the-rings-of-power-season-3-as-celeborn/)*, according to a report from TheFutonCritic.com. The showrunners and cast confirmed the first four episodes will arrive on November 11, 2026.

The panel revealed a darker tone for the upcoming season, as Sauron's armies march across Middle-earth. His obsession with forging the One Ring to achieve total domination forces the Dwarves, Elves, Men, and Wizards to form uneasy alliances in a race against time.

## Who are the new characters joining the cast?

Several new faces are joining the ensemble. Eddie Marsan will portray Thrain, the older brother of King Durin IV. Andrew Richardson plays Anarion, the younger son of Elendil and brother to Isildur. Adam Young joins as Marnûkh, a mysterious Orc who "may not be what he seems." They join the previously announced Jamie Campbell Bower as Celeborn and Zubin Varla as Khamûl the Easterling.

One of the biggest surprises was the announcement that Simon Pegg will provide the voice for the fiery Balrog, marking the first time the creature will speak. The panel also featured a surprise appearance by four armored Nazgnagôl, introduced as the Second Age precursors to the Ringwraiths.

## When will all the Season 3 episodes be released?

The release schedule for the eight-episode season is set. Following the November 11 premiere of the first four episodes, subsequent episodes will be released weekly, all exclusively on Prime Video.

The series, created by Patrick McKay and John D. Payne, is a prequel set thousands of years before the events of *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*. It follows an ensemble cast, including Morfydd Clark as Galadriel and Charlie Vickers as Sauron, as they confront the re-emergence of evil.
