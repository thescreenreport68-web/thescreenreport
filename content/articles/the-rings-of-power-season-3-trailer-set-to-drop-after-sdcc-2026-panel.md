Amazon Studios has unveiled the first look at the third season of its epic fantasy series **[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/tv/jamie-campbell-bower-joins-the-rings-of-power-season-3-as-celeborn/)**. The trailer debuted during a presentation at San Diego Comic-Con, according to Variety, and confirmed a major new addition to the cast.

Simon Pegg will voice the Balrog, a hulking magma monster destined to play a crucial role in the season's central conflict. The official logline states, “Season Three takes place at the height of the War of the Elves and Sauron, as the Dark Lord seeks to craft the One Ring that will give him the edge he needs to win the war, bind all peoples to his will – and at last rule all Middle-earth.”

## Who else is joining the cast?

Alongside returning stars like Morfydd Clark (Galadriel), Charlie Vickers (Sauron), and Robert Aramayo (Elrond), the show has expanded its ensemble for the new season. Newcomers include Jamie Campbell Bower, Eddie Marsan, Andrew Richardson, Zubin Varla, and Adam Young. The series, which takes place thousands of years before the events of *The Lord of the Rings*, continues to explore the forging of the titular rings and Sauron’s rise to power.

## Who is behind the camera for Season 3?

Season 3 is produced by showrunners J.D. Payne and Patrick McKay. They executive produce alongside Lindsey Weber, Justin Doble, Kate Hazell, and director Charlotte Brändström. Brändström is joined by directors Sanaa Hamri and Stefan Schwartz, with each helming multiple episodes of the upcoming season. The series streams exclusively on Amazon Prime Video, where it has been nominated for seven Primetime Emmys.
