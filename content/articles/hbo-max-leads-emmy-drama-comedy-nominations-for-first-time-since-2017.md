HBO Max has secured the most Emmy nominations in both the drama and comedy series categories this year, according to a report from Variety. This marks the first time the streaming platform has led both genres since 2017.

The sophomore season of the medical drama "[The Pitt](/awards/gold-derby-predicts-emmys-casting-wins-for-the-pitt-widow-s-bay-beef/)" leads all programs with 25 nominations. The comedy series "Hacks" is close behind with 24 nominations, making it the most-nominated comedy series in Emmy history. This gives HBO Max a rare opportunity to capture television's top drama and comedy prizes in the same year.

## How often do the frontrunners win?

Historical statistics suggest nomination dominance does not guarantee victory. According to Variety, among the 18 instances where a single network led nominations in both drama and comedy, the series ultimately won only four times. The most-nominated program has won its corresponding top category 56% of the time for drama, but only 42% for comedy.

## What are the odds for 'The Pitt' and 'Hacks'?

"The Pitt" enters as the reigning drama series champion and could join an elite group of shows that won for both their first and second seasons. "Hacks," which already owns the comedy series trophy for its third season, faces a steeper historical climb, as the most-nominated comedy has lost five of the past eight ceremonies.
