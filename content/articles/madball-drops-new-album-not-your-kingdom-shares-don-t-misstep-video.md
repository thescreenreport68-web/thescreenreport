New York hardcore veterans Madball released their 10th studio album, *Not Your Kingdom*, on July 24 via Nuclear Blast, according to a report from NextMosh. Alongside the album, the band debuted a music video for the blistering new single "Don't MisStep."

This release ends an eight-year wait for new studio material from the band, whose last album was 2018's *For the Cause*. The arrival of *Not Your Kingdom* is being supported by an immediate run of European tour dates, with the band set to return to North American stages in August.

## What Are the Album's Key Details?
The album represents a significant milestone and a major logistical effort for the long-running band. Here are the concrete facts:

* **Release Date:** July 24, 2026
* **Label:** Nuclear Blast
* **Track Count:** 14 songs
* **Recording Studio:** The Boneyard Studios in Nashville, Tennessee
* **Production Team:** Andrew Baylis, Aiden Thompson, and Grady Saxman
* **Mixing/Mastering:** Lee Rouse
* **Lead Single Video:** Co-directed by vocalist Freddy Cricien and Dave Causa

The music video for "Don't MisStep" is a fast-paced, high-energy clip that matches the song's 1:34 runtime. Additional filming was provided by Drew Stone and Leon Rockel.

## Who Is in Madball Now?
The band's current lineup, as listed in the announcement, features a mix of seasoned veterans and newer members. Freddy Cricien remains the iconic frontman, a role he has held since the band's inception as an Agnostic Front side project in the late 1980s. He is joined by guitarist Mike Gurnari and drummer Mike Justian. On bass is Paul Delaney, who stepped into the role following the departure of founding member Jorge "Hoya Roc" Guerra in 2023.

This configuration recorded the album in Nashville, a location noted by New Noise Magazine as an unconventional but successful choice for a hardcore record. The production team, more commonly associated with country and rock artists like [Jelly Roll](/music/jelly-roll-films-new-video-hands-up-at-san-quentin-prison/) and Luke Combs, brought a loud, crisp sound to the project.

## How Does the Album Fit Into Madball's Legacy?
As their 10th studio album, *Not Your Kingdom* arrives at a point where the band must balance their foundational sound with decades of evolution. The album's presentation, featuring a return of the band's classic logo and artwork that echoes 1994's seminal *Set It Off*, deliberately signals a nod to their roots.

The lead single "Rebel Kids" is highlighted as one of the group's catchiest anthems to date. However, some analysis suggests the record walks a line between the raw abrasion of their early work and a more refined, accessible modern hardcore style. Blabbermouth notes the lyrics offer a raw, introspective look into Cricien's perspective, marking it as perhaps their most personal release.

## What's Next for Madball?
The band is taking the album directly to their fans with an extensive touring schedule. A run of European dates began almost immediately after the July 24 release, with shows in Germany, Switzerland, and France. The tour pivots to North America in mid-August, with club dates scheduled across New York, Ontario, Quebec, and a stop at the Vans Warped Tour in Montreal.

This aggressive touring cycle is a clear statement that after nearly four decades, Madball's commitment to the road and the hardcore scene remains unwavering.
